Now a slot costs its holder one full rendezvous per session budget (≤ 120 s), silent or chatty. The second-review cut polled `next()` unconditionally and dropped the overflow itself: Arti's control loop counts the service as loaded only while its queue is non-empty (`update_suggested_effort`, `pow/v1.rs` 968–1036, every 300 s; a request leaves the queue exactly when the consumer polls, 1254–1272), so the suggested effort stayed at zero, PoW was never demanded and never ordered admission — a low-rate flood decided admission by first-come semaphores (third review, high; verified from the Arti sources). Tornect's client therefore solves only after a *fast* failure inside one `connect` (the `NOT_RECOGNIZED` retry in `review3-live2.log`), once its estimator is warm enough for the rendezvous wait to end inside the 60 s, or — on its first attempt — once the descriptor's suggested effort is nonzero, which only PoW-bearing arrivals can make it (`num_enqueued_gte_suggested`, `pow/v1.rs` 1150–1158). Under a queue-filling no-PoW flood a cold client's effort-0 request is the youngest of its class — behind every older one, and the first displaced when the 1024-deep queue overflows (`pop_first`, `pow/v1.rs` 1169–1177) — so it times out at effort 0 on every call, for as long as the flood lasts. **Residual exposure, stated**: the listener has no peer identity before the envelope's signed `HELLO`. The tighter choke point is the handshake budget: a request whose rendezvous point is dead or slow holds a permit until Arti's 2 circuit-build attempts give up or the 30 s cap fires, so ≈ 32/30 ≈ 1 dead-RP introduction per second keeps it full (the circuit budget needs ≈ 0.5 rendezvous/s, 64 per 120 s), both far below the 25/s intro rate limit. **Without PoW that is a lockout of every later client** — which is why `tor.pow_defense` defaults on and is refused rather than ignored when it cannot be honoured (M1-5). With PoW the same flood only fills the slots; everything behind them is ordered by effort, so a client that *pays* effort is served before a flood that pays none — the low-rate flood of the third review is fixed (a FIFO wait in Arti's queue, nothing dropped), but whether a client pays is the client's business, and Tornect's own client cannot yet on the unanswered path (fourth review, above); the listener takes what Arti hands it, in effort order, and cannot change that. The cost the host cannot avoid is one circuit build per admitted request (32 × 30 s of handshake budget cycled by the attacker) — paid also for a request whose client stopped waiting while it queued, since a `RendRequest` carries no receive time (`req.rs` 25–47) and the listener cannot skip stale ones; while saturated the loop logs `rendezvous budget full` once per admitted request, the host's only view of the overflow. Per-device accounting, promoting authenticated streams out of the unauthenticated budget (e.g. `OnionStream::mark_authenticated`) and the §B.3 temp-ban list belong to the envelope track, and so does the **client-side follow-up of the fourth review (M1 close)**: when the fetched descriptor advertises `pow-params`, retry inside `TorNode::connect` under a longer overall deadline so that Arti's rendezvous-completion timeout and effort ≥ 8 retry can fire (Arti takes the effort only from the descriptor and its own retries, so this most likely means a longer `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` under PoW), or carry a Tornect-level effort floor across `connect()` calls, which needs an upstream Arti request to persist or accept a client effort. Acceptance: the cold-client step of the VERIFICATION.md "PoW DoS defense" recipe (`beginning solve` logged within `CONNECT_TIMEOUT`) | Permit per accepted stream only (idle circuits would still be free to keep); a single larger semaphore (still a lockout, just bigger); a total-lifetime cap on every circuit inside the listener (would cut legitimate long sessions until authenticated promotion exists); backpressure without PoW (Arti's FIFO would grow without bound); keeping 8 handshakes (a 32-deep honest burst would have waited a full effort-update period for no gain); a configurable admission policy (each policy is only sound with the matching Arti queue) |
